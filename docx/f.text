PROJECT: AI Urban Farming Assistant

PROJECT OVERVIEW:
Build a complete, polished, responsive AI-powered urban gardening web application. It helps users identify possible plant diseases from uploaded leaf images, receive personalized plant care and watering recommendations, manage their plants, and track plant health over time.

The main demo journey must work:
1. Add a tomato plant.
2. Open its plant details.
3. Upload a tomato leaf image.
4. Display an AI disease prediction with confidence and uncertainty handling.
5. Show symptoms and basic treatment/care recommendations.
6. Display a watering recommendation based on plant type, soil condition, weather, pot size, and sunlight.
7. Save the diagnosis and care activity.
8. Show the updated plant health history on the dashboard.

YOUR ROLE:
Think and work like a senior engineer. Build a cohesive product, not a collection of disconnected screens. Use reusable components, consistent spacing, clean architecture, accessible interactions, realistic states, and maintainable code. Prioritize a working end-to-end application.

Diagnosis workflow:
1. Select an existing plant or add a new plant.
2. Upload a leaf image.
3. Show a preview and confirmation.
4. Submit the image to the backend.
5. Display an animated but restrained analysis/loading state.
6. Render the actual returned prediction.
7. Save and display the diagnosis history.

Supported example tomato classes:
- Healthy
- Early Blight
- Late Blight
- Leaf Mold
- Septoria Leaf Spot
- Uncertain / Unsupported

Important AI safety and UX requirements:
- Show “Possible diagnosis,” not a guaranteed diagnosis.
- Confidence is model confidence, not proven diagnostic accuracy.
- If the model confidence is below its configured threshold, show an uncertainty state.
- Do not invent a prediction when the backend is unavailable.
- Distinguish actual AI results from sample/demo data.
- For uncertain results, recommend taking a clearer photo and inspecting the entire plant.
- Avoid automatically recommending chemical pesticides from an uncertain image.
